Some editions of the book say (under the “Preface to the Fourth Edition”) that George updated the book before his death with “slight alterations in syntax and punctuation,” along with a couple of endnotes. As far as I can tell, the 1898 edition that this transcribtion is based off contains these. Modern editions also contain a second preface by his son, George Jr, or a completely overhauled frontmatter courtesy of the Robert Schalkenbach Foundation editions.
